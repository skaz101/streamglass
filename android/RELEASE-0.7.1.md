# StreamGlass 0.7.1

- Curated Home: Featured and Top movies/series, Netflix and HBO Max listings,
  handpicked anime, and seasonal collections near Halloween, Christmas and more.
- Settings → Home categories can switch back to installed addon catalogs.
- Vibrant indigo, iris, rose and cyan surfaces with bounded backdrop blur.
- Pull-to-refresh on phones, preserving content during Home/calendar loading.
- Swipeable featured carousel, eight-second countdown, decoded artwork
  crossfades, pause controls and reduced-motion support.

Install over the existing app to retain local account data. Android retains
package `com.streamglass.mobile` and the original preview signing certificate;
LG webOS retains `com.streamglass.tv`. Playback still uses installed addons.
Provider listings vary by country and do not imply a streaming subscription.

Automated validation is recorded in RELEASE-AUDIT.md. Physical-device frame
pacing remains a separate check.
